01 · Split the claim pass: a verdict step and a reservation transaction

Solution. A pure verdict step decides each candidate; a codec owns the denial record; a transaction owns tokens, borrow, probe, tier handles and fundings with one abandon path. _claim_pass shrinks to a driver whose lock phases are structural.

Wins

  • Verdict tests need no patching
  • Lock phases enforced by structure
