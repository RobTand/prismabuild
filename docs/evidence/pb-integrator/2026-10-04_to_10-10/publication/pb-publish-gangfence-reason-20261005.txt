No lease, token, reservation or broker format change versus the live generation 1ac00386448d. Optional record fields are added; none is required of an existing record. Three changes on that base. (1) The PrismaBuild #1537 withdrawn-claim retention carried onto SDK4 (#1533, #1534), already approved and qualified for held publication B: when a claimed action is ended by withdrawal, PoolQueue.finish retains the worker's completed cancellation as a withdrawn_attempt on the existing immutable attempt contract before entombing the claim; the original withdrawal decision is not rewritten. (2) The D31 gang-fence backfill (PR 1556, approved twice by independent review at 24d86ee1, carried as the net of that PR with identical changed lines in all five files): a ready gang member whose peers cannot yet commit lends its idle host to proven priority -10 restartable backfill, and reclaims it through the existing withdraw-and-requeue preemption BEFORE its own capacity gates, so a lent host cannot delay the gang; ordinary background preemption for gang members is kept. It adds optional fields: gang_backfill marks on claimed rows, gang_backfill_preemption on withdrawal decisions, gang_backfill_release on the filed withdrawn record, and backfill_reclaiming and backfill_preemptions on election files. Mixed-generation interval, checked in the live base code: the decision reader validates only status and address (no closed key set), the retained-withdrawal archive is write-only, and the election reader ignores extra fields; an old loop that requeues a claim carrying a mark does not strip the mark (it is not in its claim-scoped field list) and the new claim path removes it. (3) The #1547 reader-first step (PR 1558, approved by independent review at c6e7c4e2, carried with identical changed lines): _validate_worker_runtime accepts an OPTIONAL digest_primitives key in a worker attestation runtime body, normalized like the core identity and bound by runtime_sha256; any other unknown key is still refused, and a body without the key validates exactly as before. No worker emits the key in this generation: the emitter (PR 1553) merges only after this generation has converged and long-lived readers (pbmcp and any Python process that imported the old core) have been restarted. Until that restart an old reader raises CASTamperError, a RuntimeError, on a receipt carrying the new key; that error on the runtime keys means an old reader, not tampering. Emergency control: PRISMABUILD_GANG_BACKFILL=0 in a worker's environment restores the strict gang fence for that host. HONEST LIMIT: there is no finite code-derived bound on how long a GB10 takes to stop a container and return its tokens, so a stuck cleanup leaves a gang waiting; it is visible (the denial gang_waiting_for_backfill_release and recorded request and token-return times) and a live release-latency measurement is a recorded follow-up. Loops restart onto the new generation as they become idle; a running action finishes under the generation that claimed it.
