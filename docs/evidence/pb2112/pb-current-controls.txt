2: 
3: Status: source contract, **enforcement default-off**.  The fleet runs under
4: CEO decision D1 (an interim Netdata disk check) until the end-to-end proof
5: below passes and the integrator turns `enforce` on.  This replaces PR #1490;
6: its review (`pull/1490#issuecomment-5982325705`) is the specification this
7: answers, finding by finding, at the end.
8: 
9: ## The rule
10: 
11: For every physical filesystem PrismaBuild writes:
12: 
13:     free_bytes >= ceil(size_bytes / 20) + charge_bytes (+ demand_bytes)
14:     free_inodes >= ceil(size_inodes / 20)
15: 
16: `charge_bytes` is every byte allowance PrismaBuild has granted on that
17: filesystem and not yet taken back: the held tokens of the byte kinds
18: `spool_gb`, `stage_gib` and `filesystem_gib` (all GiB) in every ledger bound
19: to the filesystem.  A held token is charged whole even after its bytes are
20: written (no materialization credit), so the charge is an upper bound on the
21: bytes still to come.  The module is `src/prismabuild/filesystem_floor.py`.
22: 
23: Inodes are sampled from `statvfs` `f_files` and `f_favail` (#1535), including
24: on ZFS where byte capacity still comes from the pool dataset. A zero total

7:
